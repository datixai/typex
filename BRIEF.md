# XTyping brief

- **Product:** XTyping, a free, offline typing tutor for Windows 10 and 11 (one .exe installer, plus a portable .exe).
- **Audience:** office and lab members with slow typing speed who want to learn touch typing.
- **Reference:** typing.com, used for ideas only (lesson path, typing tests, games, progress, achievements). No brand, text or images copied.
- **Brand:** XTyping, own icon (X keycap). Deep indigo, violet and mint palette, Inter font (bundled, works offline).
- **Credit:** "Developed by Datix AI", opens https://datixai.com.
- **Accounts:** many local profiles on one PC. Username in small letters, full name, password (scrypt hashed), one-time recovery code. No cloud, no network calls. Progress stays on the PC until its data is erased. Optional export/import of progress to a file.
- **Must have:** lessons (beginner to advanced, plus numpad), timed tests, games, stats and key heatmap, achievements, daily goal and streak, tick key sounds, switchable keyboard layout (full-size with numpad, tenkeyless, laptop, laptop with numpad), light/dark theme.
- **Repo:** https://github.com/datixai/xtyping (ask before every push).
