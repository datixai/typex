\chapter{Welcome}
TypeX is a free typing tutor for Windows~10 and Windows~11. It teaches touch typing step by step, measures your speed and accuracy, and keeps you practising with games and achievements.
This guide is for testers who only have the installer. The version is 1.0.1. Your feedback will help improve the final release.

TypeX works fully offline. It has no ads, no online account and no tracking. Every person who uses the PC can have their own private profile, and all progress stays on that PC.

\chapter{Installing TypeX}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Unzip the package you received and open the folder.
\item Double-click \textbf{TypeX-Setup-1.0.1.exe} (about 100~MB).
\item Windows may display a dialog \textbf{Windows protected your PC}. Click \textbf{More info} and then \textbf{Run anyway}. This appears because the app is new and not yet code-signed.
\item Choose an install folder or accept the default. Click \textbf{Install}, then \textbf{Finish}.
\item A shortcut called \textbf{TypeX} appears on the desktop and in the Start menu.
\item No administrator password, internet connection or other software is needed.
\item To uninstall, open \texttt{Settings} $\rightarrow$ \texttt{Apps} $\rightarrow$ \texttt{Installed apps} $\rightarrow$ \texttt{TypeX} and click \textbf{Uninstall}.
\end{enumerate}

\section{System Requirements}
\begin{itemize}
\item Windows~10 or Windows~11, 64-bit.
\item About 350~MB of free disk space.
\item Any keyboard: desktop, laptop, with or without a number pad.
\end{itemize}

\chapter{Creating Your Profile}
The first time TypeX starts, it asks you to create a profile.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Enter your \textbf{Full name}.
\item Choose a \textbf{Username}. Only small letters, numbers, dot and underscore are allowed, for example \texttt{sara.khan}.
\item Enter a \textbf{Password} twice, then click \textbf{Create profile and start}.
\item A \textbf{recovery code} is shown once. Write it down and keep it safe. Because there is no online account, this code is the only way to reset a forgotten password.
\item Pick the keyboard that looks most like yours. You can change it later in Settings.
\end{enumerate}
Other people on the same PC click \textbf{New profile} on the start screen to make their own. To sign in later, click your profile and enter your password.

\section{Forgot Your Password}
On the sign-in screen click \textbf{Forgot password?}, enter your username, the recovery code and a new password. TypeX then shows a new recovery code: save it again.

\chapter{The Dashboard}
After signing in you see the Dashboard. It shows your average speed (WPM, words per minute), accuracy, lessons done and day streak.
The large card shows your next lesson: click \textbf{Continue} to carry on. The ring on the right shows today's practice time against your daily goal, and your level and XP.
At the bottom you see your speed trend and recent activity.
\shot{dashboard.png}{The Dashboard.}

The menu on the left opens \textbf{Dashboard}, \textbf{Lessons}, \textbf{Typing Test}, \textbf{Games}, \textbf{Progress}, \textbf{Achievements} and \textbf{Settings}. Your name and level are at the bottom; the arrow button signs you out.

\chapter{Lessons}
TypeX has 49 lessons in four tracks:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Beginner}: every letter, row by row, starting with the home row (F and J).
\item \textbf{Intermediate}: numbers, punctuation and short sentences.
\item \textbf{Advanced}: symbols, email and web addresses, code and paragraphs.
\item \textbf{Number Pad}: ten-key typing for data entry.
\end{itemize}
Follow the path in order, or click any lesson to jump to it. Each lesson earns up to three stars.
\shot{lessons.png}{The Lessons page with the four tracks.}

\section{Taking a Lesson}
A lesson introducing new keys first shows which finger to use. Press \textbf{Space} to start, then type the text shown.
\begin{itemize}
\item The next key glows on the on-screen keyboard, and the hand guide lights up the finger to use.
\item Correct letters turn white. A mistake turns red and makes a short sound.
\item Live speed, accuracy and time are shown at the top right.
\item Press \textbf{Esc} at any time to restart the lesson.
\end{itemize}
\shot{lesson.png}{A lesson in progress with the glowing next key and the finger guide.}

\section{Results}
At the end you see your stars, speed, accuracy, time and XP. Click \textbf{Try again} or \textbf{Next lesson}, or press \textbf{Enter} for the next lesson.
The minimise button (a short line) at the top right minimises the popup so you can look at the text again; \textbf{Show results} brings it back. The \textbf{X} button, or \textbf{Esc}, closes it and returns to the lessons.
\shot{result.png}{The result popup.}

\section{Beginner and Pro Modes}
Use the switch at the top of the Lessons page.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Beginner}: key and finger hints, and you fix mistakes as you go.
\item \textbf{Pro}: no hints, Backspace is blocked, the text is longer, and three stars need 98\% accuracy and a higher speed. Pro stars are saved separately and give extra XP.
\end{itemize}
After you earn three stars in Beginner mode, the result popup offers \textbf{Try Pro}.
\shot{lessons_pro.png}{Pro mode, marked with PRO on every lesson.}

\chapter{Typing Test}
Open \textbf{Typing Test} to measure your real speed.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Choose the length: 30 seconds, 1, 2, 3 or 5 minutes.
\item Choose the text: \textbf{Stories}, \textbf{Common words}, \textbf{Code} or \textbf{My text} (paste any text you like).
\item Click \textbf{Start test}. The timer starts with your first key.
\end{enumerate}
The result shows WPM, raw WPM, accuracy, errors and the keys you should practise. Your best score and recent tests appear on the right.
\shot{test_setup.png}{Typing test options and your test history.}

\chapter{Games}
Games help you practise while having fun. Click a game, then \textbf{Play}.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Meteor Words}: words fall from space. Type each word before it reaches the ground.
\item \textbf{Balloon Pop}: letters float up on balloons. Press the key to pop them. Great for beginners.
\item \textbf{Speed Sprint}: race three robot cars to the finish line by typing a short passage.
\end{itemize}
\shot{games.png}{The Games page.}

\chapter{Progress and Achievements}
\textbf{Progress} shows your best and average speed, accuracy, total practice time, speed and accuracy charts, practice time for the last 14 days and your full history.
The \textbf{key heatmap} colours every key by accuracy: green keys are accurate, yellow and red keys need practice.
\shot{progress_heat.png}{The key heatmap and practice history.}

\textbf{Achievements} lists 19 badges, for example finishing lessons, reaching 50 WPM or practising 7 days in a row. Unlocked badges show the date you earned them.

\chapter{Settings}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Keyboard layout}: Full-size (with number pad), Tenkeyless, Laptop (compact) or Laptop with number pad.
\item \textbf{Sound}: key sound (Mechanical tick, Typewriter, Soft tap, Bubble or Off), volume and the mistake sound.
\item \textbf{Practice}: stop on mistakes, show the on-screen keyboard and hand guide, target speed for three stars and your daily goal.
\item \textbf{Theme}: Dark or Light.
\item \textbf{Profile}: change your full name or password.
\item \textbf{Data on this PC}: save a backup file of your progress, or load one, for example to move to another PC. You can also delete your profile here.
\end{itemize}

\chapter{Tips for Faster Typing}
\begin{itemize}
\item Keep your fingers on the home row: A S D F and J K L ;. Feel the bumps on F and J.
\item Look at the screen, not at your hands.
\item Accuracy first. Speed comes by itself once your fingers know the way.
\item Practise 10 to 15 minutes every day. Short daily practice beats one long session.
\item Sit up straight, keep your wrists relaxed and take short breaks.
\end{itemize}

\chapter{Troubleshooting}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Windows protected your PC}: click \textbf{More info}, then \textbf{Run anyway}.
\item \textbf{Antivirus blocks the installer}: allow TypeX, or ask your IT person to allow it.
\item \textbf{No sound}: check Settings $\rightarrow$ Sound and the Windows volume.
\item \textbf{Keys do not match my keyboard}: choose your layout in Settings $\rightarrow$ Keyboard layout.
\item \textbf{Progress could not be saved}: the disk may be full. Free some space and try again.
\item \textbf{Forgot password}: use \textbf{Forgot password?} and your recovery code.
\end{itemize}
Profiles are stored in \texttt{\%APPDATA\%\textbackslash{}TypeX}. Uninstalling keeps them, in case you reinstall.
